## Offline experience target

- `/dashboard/sync-center` → `src/app/dashboard/sync-center/page.tsx` — pending local changes, failed sync items, last-synced status and manual sync.
- `/dashboard` → role-aware dashboards selected by `src/app/dashboard/DashboardRouter.tsx`.
- `/dashboard/attendance` — daily class register with offline queue support.
- `/dashboard/grades` — marks entry with offline queue support.
- `/dashboard/fees` — payments and student balances with cached reads and offline queue support.
